# Product invariants
- Student UI: Turkish, mobile-first, age-appropriate for 13–14, lively without feeling childish or distracting; avoid monotonous repeated-card layouts.
- Parent and teacher are one unified adult/coach experience with permission-based detail.
- Each of six LGS branches has one experienced coach evaluation tab; weekly reports are delivered as coach-reviewed assessments.
- Wrong-question photo capture and curated YouTube lessons are core capabilities.
- Teachers must use their own browsers remotely; use authenticated hosting or private-network access, never raw public port forwarding.
- Explain every mastery/intervention status with evidence; no opaque score labels.
